scCS v.0.8
==========

**scCS** quantifies supervised cell-fate commitment at a manually annotated
furcation. It combines an RNA-velocity transition graph, a continuous ordering,
and user-supplied root and fate annotations while keeping scientific dynamics
separate from the standardized root-plus-star display.

New users should begin with :doc:`introduction`, then follow :doc:`quickstart`
and one of the complete pancreas or Schwann tutorials.
