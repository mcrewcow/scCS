PairScorer
==========

Learn to compare two conditions on a shared annotated furcation and summarize
effects over independent biological replicates. The pancreas and Schwann
examples use controlled pseudo-conditions. For observed comparisons in patient,
culture and developmental data, see :doc:`tutorials_practical`.
